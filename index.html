👑🌙 The Knight and the Hidden Crown

A Story of Preme & Priya ✨

In a kingdom where crowns hide secrets and ancient magic sleeps beneath forgotten stones, a selfless knight named Preme is ordered to protect a mysterious slave named Priya. ⚔️🌹

She carries secrets she cannot remember, while he carries memories he cannot understand. 🕯️

Between them grows a quiet love—slow, elegant, and deeper than either of them expected. ❤️

But in Valedorn, nothing is what it seems… and some truths are dangerous enough to destroy a kingdom. 👑🌑
