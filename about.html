Behind the story

A house made of

“The sea does not erase a thing. It only teaches the shore to keep it differently.”

The House at Low Tide is an ongoing gothic mystery about inheritance, grief, and the architecture of secrets. New chapters arrive as the story finds its shape.

About the author

Elian Vale writes stories about strange thresholds and ordinary people brave enough to cross them. This is a fictional work in progress, published one chapter at a time.
